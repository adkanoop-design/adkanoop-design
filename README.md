## Selected work at Northern Trust

**FundMaster Modernization — $2B programme** · Data Governance and Data Domain Lead
Owned governance framework design, legacy IBM AS400 to cloud-native Fresche migration, data quality validation
and target-state schema design for the firm's custody book of record. Wrote the SQL and DB2 reconciliation and
parallel-run logic certifying record-level parity before production cutover.

**Global Settlement Instructions (GSI)** · Data & Analytics Lead
Owned the analytical workstream on a settlement-instruction platform that enriched trades, matched counterparty
SSIs and flagged mismatches *before affirmation* — the point in the trade lifecycle where intervention still
prevents a fail. **99% straight-through processing across all markets, including exotic markets.**

**Transaction Hub — $200M cloud modernization** · Requirements Workstream Lead
Authored the canonical SWIFT MT/MX and ISO 20022 message contracts across Trade, Account, Product and Broker
enrichment on a 30-person core team. Live in 2018 and credited at year end with $20M in new revenue.

**Legal Contract Risk Analytics**
Identified duplicated cost in the review of client Will and Trust agreements and delivered in-house data
solutions replacing both a manual process and an external vendor platform — credited by the Head of Legal
Compliance and Risk with **$20M in avoided subscription cost**.

**Conversational AI programme (2015–2017)**
Business requirements and delivery leadership on Northern Trust's first conversational AI, built on **IBM Watson
and Microsoft** platforms: internal employee chatbot, extended to client servicing partners, then voice biometric
authentication and speech analytics.
