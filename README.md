## Selected work at Northern Trust

**Entity Master — Tier 1 Application, within Enterprise Data Modernization (2016–2017)** · Originator; capability and framework design through implementation
Built to strengthen **transaction enrichment and straight-through processing**. Transactions were failing party
validation and queuing for manual intervention, so the platform enriched and validated party data at the moment a
transaction invokes validation, clearing the queue at source. Analysis of the party data domain during design
exposed a wider exposure: fragmented counterparty identity was obscuring true aggregate exposure to a **parent
entity whose sub-entities held multi-layered relationships** across the firm. I built the business case on that
basis, took it to the approving committee including the **executive steering committee for annual capital
allocation**, won funding against competing firmwide proposals, and delivered it as a **Tier 1 application** leading
a 10-person cross-functional team. Sourced and built in-house a **real-time data pipeline** from Bloomberg, SWIFT
and GLEIF resolving parent-to-subsidiary entity profiles at transaction time. Remediated **7,000+ legal entity
identifiers** — duplicate entities, parent-child hierarchy errors and credit classification errors — within a
submitting population of **33,000+ counterparties and investment managers**. Scoped to enterprise impact across
**Global Securities Services**: accounting, entity/party, assets, global trade and settlement, custody operations,
client servicing and asset servicing. Accurate legal entity identity is upstream of asset attribution, which makes
it a **client asset segregation and record-keeping control**, a **credit concentration** control and a **BCBS 239**
risk data aggregation control — and the identity foundation on which **AML, KYC and sanctions screening** depend.

**Index Master** · Lead Business Analyst & Lead Data Specialist
Resolved instrument and trading-venue identity where clients submitted their own reference data, validating vendor
feeds from **Bloomberg, PolarLake, Reuters, IDC and Markit EDM** against golden-source products rather than treating
vendor data as authoritative. Venue ambiguity flows directly into valuation, settlement routing and client reporting.

**Transaction Hub — $200M cloud modernization** · Requirements, Data Design & Release Delivery Lead
Led requirements, data design and release delivery for end-to-end transaction processing across five inbound
channels into a single canonical message standard on Cloud Foundry microservices.
Authored the **canonical message specifications and data contracts** — SWIFT MT/MX and ISO 20022 payload structures,
field-level mappings, validation rules and enrichment logic across trade, account, party, product and broker —
consuming governed master data through **Enterprise MDM and Product Entity Management**. Achieved **100%
straight-through processing end to end**; live in 2018 and credited at year end with **$20M in new revenue** from
client mandates the capability made winnable.

**FundMaster Modernization — $2B program** · Data Governance and Data Domain Lead
Owned governance framework design, legacy IBM AS400 to cloud-native Fresche migration, data quality validation and
target-state schema design for Northern Trust's global custody transaction processing engine and final golden-source
accounting book of record — on which all external and internal transactions are processed and ledgered, at a firm
reporting **$20 trillion in assets under custody and administration**. Wrote the SQL and DB2 reconciliation and
parallel-run logic certifying **record-level parity before production cutover**, raised enterprise data quality
**25%** on critical data element scorecards for completeness, accuracy and timeliness, and drove EUC remediation
strengthening BCBS 239 posture.

**Legal Contract Risk Analytics** · Lead Business Analyst & Lead Data Specialist
**Contract risk assessment.** Assessed the risk held in client **Will and Trust agreements**, among the least
structured and most sensitive documents the bank holds. Read the contract data directly, derived the governing risk
positions, and built in-house solutions reproducing them from governed internal sources — retiring both a heavy
manual review process and an external legal data subscription. A **build-versus-buy risk decision** delivered for
the **Head of Legal Compliance and Risk**, who credited it with **$20M in avoided multi-year cost**. Extended
documented domain coverage into fiduciary trust, estate, legal, compliance and risk.

**Peek-Through Accounting Services — AXA Rosenberg** · Business Analytics Lead and Program Lead
Brought in after a strategically important master-feeder accounting initiative had escalated under complex NAV,
look-through and allocation challenges. The client's mother-baby fund structure required NAV at both feeder and
master level, look-through to the master's underlying holdings, and allocation of income, expense and realized and
unrealized gains across feeders — where a structural error at any level propagates into every downstream number.
Diagnosed across accounting, data, technology and operational process rather than treating individual breaks in
isolation, established end-to-end data lineage, introduced analytical controls and exception-based monitoring, and
drove remediation toward a scalable operating model. Improved accounting transparency, strengthened data and
reconciliation controls, reduced manual intervention and mitigated client, operational and financial risk.

**Global Settlement Instructions (GSI)** · Data & Analytics Lead
Owned the analytical workstream on a settlement-instruction platform that enriched trades, matched counterparty
SSIs and flagged mismatches *before affirmation* — the point in the trade lifecycle where intervention still
prevents a fail. Defined the business problem, data requirements, features and target outputs for predictive models
built in Python by the engineering team. **Straight-through processing raised from a 70% baseline to 99% across all
markets, including exotic markets** — a 29-point gain removing manual intervention on roughly a third of settlement
instructions.

**One Lot Account Engine (OLAE) — $4M program** · Originator, Lead Data Specialist
Originated and delivered a dedicated **tax lot calculation and accounting engine** integrated with FundMaster,
**designed to process transactions of any value** — removing a **$1 billion ceiling** that had been forcing
segmentation of high-value transactions, rather than raising it. Led the business requirements for the tax lot
accounting logic: **cost basis method handling** (FIFO, LIFO, average cost, specific identification), **wash sale
detection** across the 61-day window with basis adjustment and loss disallowance, **corporate action basis
reallocation** across spinoffs, mergers and stock splits, **holding period tracking** driving short- versus
long-term gain classification, and **covered versus non-covered lot treatment**. Delivery **decommissioned IMS**,
the legacy domestic accounting system.

**TLM Reconciliation Platform** · Program Manager, Business Technology Change
Led enhancements and client-driven change on the Transaction Lifecycle Maintenance engine — ingesting client and
investment-manager transaction feeds several times daily and reconciling them against internally processed
transactions. TLM's **5pm CST cycle ran against FundMaster's 7pm close**: the window in which every break had to
clear before the custody book of record and client reporting were finalized. Reconciliation integrity underpins
client asset protection under **UK CASS**, with equivalent daily control expectations in the US and APAC.

**Corporate Actions — Ex-Date Change (2023)** · Led the Business Analytics Program
Converted corporate actions entitlement processing to the **T+1 ex-date convention** — aligning ex-date with record
date — plus automated handling of issuer and market-driven ex-date revisions requiring recalculation of entitlements
already computed. Delivered ahead of the May 2024 US T+1 go-live. Incorrect conversion would have produced wrong
entitlements on every dividend and distribution from day one.

**Client Reporting Passport** · Test Authority
